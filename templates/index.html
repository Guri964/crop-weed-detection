AI Modules

Two Models, Purpose-Built for the Field

Each module is trained and tuned for a specific job, from close-up leaf analysis to wide-angle field scanning.

Module 1

Pest & Stress Detection

Upload a close-up image of a plant leaf. Our system identifies the crop and any leaf disease, covering 14 crop species and their common diseases.

  • Identifies 26 diseases and 12 healthy classes across 14 crops
  • Provides root cause analysis
  • Suggests treatment and prevention
Module 2

Weed Detection

Upload an aerial or overhead image of your crop field. Our YOLOv8 model evaluates the remote sensing data to pinpoint unwanted vegetation.

  • Processes full field imagery
  • Generates precise bounding boxes
  • Highlights exact weed locations
