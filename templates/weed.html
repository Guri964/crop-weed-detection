Field Weed Detection

Upload an aerial or overhead image of your field. Our YOLOv8 model scans the full image and marks every detected weed with a precise bounding box.

For Best Results

  • Capture the image from directly overhead, drone or elevated shot.
  • Shoot in good daylight for clear crop and weed contrast.
  • Cover as much of the field as possible in a single frame.
  • Avoid heavy motion blur or extreme angles.

What It Outputs

Weed-only Boxes Weed & Crop Counts Downloadable Result
How it works: your field image is passed through a YOLOv8 detector with two classes, crop and weed. Only weed detections above the confidence threshold are drawn, so crop plants are never boxed. A single image is not a field-wide survey.
