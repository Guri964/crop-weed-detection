For Best Results

  • Take the photo in natural daylight, avoiding harsh shadows.
  • Fill the frame with a single leaf, held flat and in focus.
  • Include any visible spots, discoloration, or damage clearly.
  • Use a plain background where possible for cleaner results.

What It Detects

Leaf Spot Blight Insect Damage Nutrient Stress Fungal Growth + more
How it works: your image is converted from RGB to HSV color space, then passed through a customized MobileNetV2 network trained to recognize 15 pest, disease, and stress classes.
